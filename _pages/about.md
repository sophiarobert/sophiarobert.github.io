      <h1 class="hero-title">Visual perception and <span>cortical organization.</span></h1>
      <p class="lead">I am <strong>Sophie Robert</strong>, a postdoctoral researcher in the Visual Neuroscience Training Program at New York University, working with <strong>Jonathan Winawer</strong>. My research examines how the anatomical and functional organization of visual cortex constrains perception and contributes to individual differences in visual performance.</p>
      <p>My current work focuses on peripheral vision, including visual crowding and natural-scene metamer discrimination. I combine psychophysical measurements, computational observer models, and neuroimaging to investigate links between cortical organization and behavior.</p>
